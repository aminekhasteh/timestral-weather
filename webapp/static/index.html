      How it works

      Timestral is a 28-million-parameter time-series model from Mistral AI. It was never trained on this place: it reads the last 60 days of hourly temperature and forecasts the next 168 hours.

      It also reads the weather service’s forecast as a hint about what’s coming, and works out from the recent past how far to trust that hint here.

      In a year-long test in Montreal, this was about 15% more accurate than the weather service alone, with the biggest gains in spring and summer and in the first 24 hours.

      Observed temperatures are Open-Meteo’s hourly analysis for these coordinates, not readings from a single weather station. The shaded band is Timestral’s own 10–90% range; in a year-long Montreal test it held about 7 in 10 outcomes, so treat it as a slightly narrow guide.
